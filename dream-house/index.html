The Studio

From concept

Dream House Design was founded in 2021 with a simple belief: construction should be reliable, transparent, and stress-free. What began as a dedicated team for residential builds has grown into a trusted full-service construction practice.

We handle everything under one roof: design consultation, structural engineering, material procurement, on-site execution, quality checks, and final handover — so you never have to coordinate between multiple vendors.

Our Process

From site visit to final handover.

01 — Consult

Consultation & Site Visit

Understanding your vision, budget, and analyzing plot dimensions, soil, and site conditions.

02 — Design

Architecture & Planning

Detailed floor layouts, 2D/3D elevations, and structural planning before building begins.

03 — Estimate

Transparent Budget & Approvals

Clear cost estimation with zero hidden charges and complete support for municipal permits.

04 — Build

Structural & Civil Execution

Foundation laying, framing, masonry, electrical, plumbing, and strict quality checks.

05 — Handover

Inspection & Key Handover

Final inspection, defect rectification, and clean handover of your ready-to-move property.
